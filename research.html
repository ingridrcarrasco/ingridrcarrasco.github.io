Exoplanet Habitability

My research interests focus on the evolution of planetary environments and the physical processes that influence habitability, including atmospheric change, stellar forcing, climate stability, and surface conditions.

Planetary Atmospheres

I am interested in how atmospheric composition, radiative balance, and long-term planetary evolution affect whether a world can support surface liquid water.
