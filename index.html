I am a planetary science student interested in exoplanet habitability, planetary atmospheres, astrobiology, and observational astronomy.

Research Interests

Current Focus

My academic interests center on how planetary environments evolve over time, especially the physical and atmospheric processes that influence whether a planet can maintain or regain habitability.
